# Event Templates Index

## Overview
The USGA Event Registration Form Generator provides **17 pre-configured event templates** designed to standardize registration flows across diverse youth, adult, servitude, and national events. This index catalogs each template's primary purpose, target audience, typical duration, and operational characteristics.

---

## Template Catalog

### 1. Student Spiritual Convocation (SSC)
* **Target Audience:** Youth in Grades 4–12.
* **Duration:** 4–7 days (Summer / Winter).
* **Key Features:**
  * Dynamic title generation by Region, Season, Year, and Classes (e.g., *East Region Summer 2026 E2/J1 SSC*).
  * Dynamic class filtering: Only the selected classes (E2, J1, J2) appear on the form.
  * Minor safeguarding: Captures Age (1-99 validation), Parent/Guardian name, and Minor's Email.
  * Complete travel logistics branching (Flight + Train/Bus) and sleeping bag options.

### 2. Young Adult Spiritual Convocation (YASC)
* **Target Audience:** Working youth, college graduates, and young adults.
* **Duration:** 4–5 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Work & college status tracking (`Are you in: College/Working`).
  * End-to-end travel logistics (Flight, Train/Bus, Car).
  * Church lodging and pre/post-event accommodation tracking.

### 3. College Youth Spiritual Convocation (CYSC)
* **Target Audience:** College and university students.
* **Duration:** 4–5 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Minor safeguarding enabled to protect incoming freshmen under 18.
  * Dress code agreement and servitude interest tracking.
  * Complete flight and train/bus travel coordination.

### 4. National Youth Theological Seminar (NYTS) - All Years
* **Target Audience:** High school seniors and college-aged youth pursuing intensive theological study.
* **Duration:** 2–3 weeks.
* **Key Features:**
  * Rigorous theological seminar workflow.
  * On-site hosting church lodging tracking (`I will be staying at the hosting church.`).
  * Volunteer servitude role selection and complete travel logistics.

### 5. National Adult Theological Seminar (NATS)
* **Target Audience:** Adult church members and fellowship leaders.
* **Duration:** 5–7 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Birth year tracking and NATS completed years (`0`, `1`, `2`, `3`, `4+`).
  * Church lodging confirmation and travel logistics.

### 6. Online National Adult Theological Seminar (OATS)
* **Target Audience:** Adult church members attending remotely.
* **Duration:** 3–5 days (Virtual).
* **Key Features:**
  * Streamlined virtual registration.
  * No travel logistics tabs generated.
  * Optional pre/post lodging for members gathering locally.

### 7. National Evangelical Workers' Workshop (NEWW)
* **Target Audience:** Evangelical coordinators, personal evangelism workers, and ministers.
* **Duration:** 3–4 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * In-person vs. Online attendance selection (`I will attend...`).
  * Complete flight and train/bus logistics.

### 8. National Marriage Coordinators' Workshop (NMCW)
* **Target Audience:** Marriage ministry coordinators and counseling teams.
* **Duration:** 3–4 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Adult-only questions (all minor fields automatically excluded).
  * Travel logistics and host church lodging.

### 9. Sacred Music Camp (SMC)
* **Target Audience:** Youth vocalists and instrumentalists.
* **Duration:** 4–6 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Minor safeguarding enabled.
  * Musical aptitude tracking: voice parts, instrument brought, music background.
  * Dress code agreement.

### 10. Southwest Region Music Ministry Program (MMP)
* **Target Audience:** Regional youth music students.
* **Duration:** 3–5 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Regional musical servitude program.
  * Inbound and outbound flight tracking.

### 11. Ministry Volunteer Program (MVP) Workshop
* **Target Audience:** Youth and young adult ministry volunteers.
* **Duration:** 3–4 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Servitude skills inventory (AV, cooking, hymn leading, translation).
  * Full travel and lodging logistics.

### 12. Pioneer Volunteer Ministry Program (PMVP)
* **Target Audience:** Pioneer evangelism and outreach workers.
* **Duration:** 5–7 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Adult servitude profile with birth year and ministry skills.
  * Host church lodging and travel logistics.

### 13. National Youth Servitude Training (NYST)
* **Target Audience:** Active youth leaders and servitude trainees.
* **Duration:** 4–5 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Leadership servitude preparation.
  * Full travel logistics and host church accommodation.

### 14. National Youth Fellowship (NYF)
* **Target Audience:** College youth and young adult fellowship members.
* **Duration:** 3–4 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * College/working status.
  * Streamlined flight logistics.

### 15. USGA Ministers Workshop
* **Target Audience:** Full-time ordained ministers and pastors.
* **Duration:** 3–5 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Pastoral workshop registration.
  * Full flight and train/bus travel coordination.

### 16. National Church Conference (NCC)
* **Target Audience:** Church delegates, pastors, board members, and observers.
* **Duration:** 3–4 days.
* **Key Features:**
  * Auto-injects Official Eligibility Disclaimer ("This is not an open conference...").
  * Delegate vs. Observer designation.
  * Full flight and train/bus logistics.

### 17. Online National Sacred Music Workshop
* **Target Audience:** Choir directors, pianists, and vocalists attending remotely.
* **Duration:** 2–3 days (Virtual).
* **Key Features:**
  * Virtual registration.
  * Zoom familiarity tracking and training requests.
