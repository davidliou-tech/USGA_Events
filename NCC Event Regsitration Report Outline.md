# USGA Event Registration System: NCC Presentation Outline

This document outlines a 7-slide presentation for NCC delegates, focusing on the user experience for **Event Coordinators**, **Local Host Churches**, and **Registrants**, along with recommended screenshots to capture for each slide.

---

## Slide 1: Title & The Vision
* **Headline:** USGA Event Registration System: Simplifying Event Coordination & Host Church Hospitality
* **Key Points:**
  * Built to eliminate manual spreadsheet setup, broken formulas, and repetitive administrative work.
  * Turns hours of form and roster building into a **15-second setup** so coordinators and host churches can focus on fellowship and care.
* **📸 Screenshot Suggestion:**
  * A clean graphic or split view showing **1 Click → 4 Instant Assets** (Live Google Form, Automated Response Workbook, Public Volunteer Driver Sheet, and Shared Drive Folder).

---

## Slide 2: Fast, Standardized Setup for Event Coordinators
* **Headline:** Launch a Complete Event in Minutes
* **Key Points:**
  * **17 Ready-to-Use Templates:** Pre-loads questions for SSC, NYMC, NCC, Ministers Workshops, and regional events.
  * **One-Click Safeguards & Bilingual Support:** Toggle on **Includes Minors** (automatically adds parental consent & emergency fields) and **Traditional Chinese** translations.
  * **Smart Travel Branching:** Automatically configures flight, train/bus, and local airport options based on the host location.
* **📸 Screenshot Suggestions:**
  * **Primary:** The **Form Generator Wide Window** (`Sidebar.html`) showing the *Event Template* dropdown, the *Includes Minors* / *Bilingual* toggles, and the searchable field checklist.
  * **Inset / Callout:** The green **"Event Generated Successfully!"** popup showing the one-click links to the Live Form, Response Sheet, Public Driver Sheet, and Drive Folder.

---

## Slide 3: Better Experience for Registrants & Families
* **Headline:** Smooth, Family-Friendly Registration
* **Key Points:**
  * **Multi-Child Support:** Parents can register multiple children under a single email address—each child gets their own row on rooming and transit lists.
  * **Instant Confirmation & Self-Service Edits:** Registrants receive an official confirmation email with a unique link to update flight changes or cancel if plans shift.
  * **Automatic Waitlist Handling:** If an event hits capacity, extra registrants are placed on a waitlist and held off active rooming/driver sheets until promoted.
* **📸 Screenshot Suggestions:**
  * **Left:** A sample **Bilingual Google Form** header and travel selection question.
  * **Right:** A sample **Confirmation Email** highlighting the registration summary and the *"Edit Your Response"* link.

---

## Slide 4: Hands-Free Lifecycle Control
* **Headline:** Live Monitoring & Capacity Management
* **Key Points:**
  * **Scheduled Open & Close:** Set exact start/end dates (with clear local timezone labels) so forms open and close automatically.
  * **Real-Time Status Dashboard:** View live registration counts, remaining capacity, and open/scheduled/closed status at a glance.
  * **On-the-Fly Adjustments:** Extend deadlines, raise capacity caps, or force-close registration anytime without touching code.
* **📸 Screenshot Suggestion:**
  * The **Manage Active Events** panel (`ManageEventsSidebar.html`) showing a live event with the green `OPEN` badge, current registration count vs. max capacity, and the quick-action buttons.

---

## Slide 5: Host Church Hospitality — Room Inventory & Lodging
* **Headline:** Stress-Free Rooming for the Host Church
* **Key Points:**
  * **Define or Import Rooms in Seconds:** Host churches list (or one-click import) their buildings, rooms, gender designations, and bed limits on the `Room Inventory` tab.
  * **Decluttered `Lodging` Roster:** Automatically filters to **only** attendees staying at the host church.
  * **Live Capacity & Cancellation Tracking:** Selecting a room from the dropdown updates remaining bed counts in real time (**Green** = open, **Yellow** = full, **Red** = over capacity) and lists assigned occupants automatically.
* **📸 Screenshot Suggestions:**
  * **Top:** The **`Room Inventory` tab** showing the green input columns alongside the blue auto-calculated columns (`Assigned Count`, color-coded `Remaining Capacity`, and `Assigned Occupants` live list).
  * **Bottom:** The **`Lodging` tab** showing the `Room Assignment` dropdown menu open and a `CANCELLED` (strikethrough) row demonstrating how cancelled stays automatically free up a bed.

---

## Slide 6: Airport & Transit Coordination (With Attendee Privacy)
* **Headline:** Smart Airport Rides & Privacy-Safe Volunteer Signups
* **Key Points:**
  * **Chronological Flight & Train Grouping:** Arrivals and departures auto-sort by date and time, grouping flights landing within 30 minutes and keeping families in the same vehicle.
  * **Privacy-Safe Public Driver Sheet:** Local church volunteers claim rides on a separate sheet showing **masked passenger names only** (`David L.`)—no phone numbers or emails exposed.
  * **One-Click Dispatch Text:** Coordinators stay in full control—copy a pre-written SMS/WhatsApp/LINE message with passenger details and send it directly to the confirmed driver.
* **📸 Screenshot Suggestions:**
  * **Side-by-Side Comparison:**
    * **Volunteer View (Public Driver Signup Sheet):** Highlighting masked passenger names (`David L., Grace C.`), vehicle slots (`Car 1 of 2`), and the editable green Driver columns.
    * **Coordinator View (`Pickup` Tab):** Highlighting the synced driver name, the `Coordinator Status` dropdown (`Confirmed / Contacted`), and the `One-Click Dispatch SMS Text` cell.

## Slide 7: Real-Time Visibility for Pastors, Kitchen & Check-In
* **Headline:** Instant Numbers for Every Ministry Team
* **Key Points:**
  * **Check-In & Kitchen (`Logistics` Tab):** Master roster with visual badges for on-site vs. commuting attendees, dietary/medical allergies, emergency contacts, and fellowship group assignments.
  * **Leadership Overview (`Summary Dashboard` & `Airport Summary`):** Instant counts of total attendance, meals, room capacity vs. assigned beds, sleep habits, and arrivals per airport.
  * **Simple "Color Rule" for Volunteers:** **Green/Yellow columns** = safe for volunteers to edit; **Blue columns** = automated by the system.
* **📸 Screenshot Suggestions:**
  * **Left:** The **`Summary Dashboard` tab** showing real-time headcount, lodging, and dietary summary tables.
  * **Right:** A close-up of the **`Logistics` tab** showing the soft-green `Staying at Host Church?` badges, dietary notes, and yellow `Group/Team` column with a hover instruction note visible.

## 💡 Quick Tips for Capturing Your Screenshots
1. **Use Dummy Data with Realistic Scenarios:** Create 5–6 sample registrations that include:
   * A parent registering two siblings under one email (to show multi-child handling).
   * Two people landing within 20 minutes of each other (to show combined airport pickup slots).
   * One person who switched to self-arranged lodging (to show the red `CANCELLED` strikethrough row).
2. **Show a Hover Tooltip:** When screenshotting `Room Inventory` or `Lodging`, hover your mouse over one of the green or yellow column headers so delegates can see the built-in helper notes made for non-technical volunteers.
